our story

The original pack is back

BarksBuds began as BarksBarks, a friendly little community where dog owners and their dogs found each other. Our scruffy terrier has been part of it from the start. Now we're bringing it back, rebuilt for today, with the same idea at its heart: a group of friends that love their dogs.
