the story

The original pack is back

BarksBuds started life as BarksBarks, a friendly little community where dog owners and their dogs found each other. The terrier on our logo has been with us since then. Now we're bringing it back, rebuilt for today, with the same idea at its heart: a group of friends that love their dogs.
