site's voice: plain, local, and about the dogs. Lily is the only sample dog
name used in any demo account or screenshot.

(30 characters exactly.)

(80 characters exactly.)

## Full description (both stores)

BarksBuds is a free community for dog people in NYC, Long Island and Westchester.

Find a dog run near you, see who's there, and meet up with other owners. Create a
profile for your pup, join a pack, and get reminders for meetups you said you'd
go to — only if you turn notifications on.

• Dog run map for the city, Long Island and Westchester, with a "near me" sort
  you start yourself
• Check in at a run so other dogs can see who's there now
• Meetups and packs, including a month calendar and a calendar subscription
• Local picks: owner-voted vets, groomers and walkers, by town
• Photos of your dog (we strip hidden location data from every upload)
• Optional notifications for meetup reminders and check-ins

BarksBuds is free. We never sell or share your information. You can delete your
account any time. Questions? hello@barksbuds.app

Privacy policy: https://barksbuds.app/privacy
Terms: https://barksbuds.app/terms
